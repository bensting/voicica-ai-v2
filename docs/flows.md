| 18 | Stuck-job sweep (timeout → failed, release hold) | **Defined** (responsibility; exact timeout threshold open) | [ADR 0007](decisions/0007-scheduled-tasks-module.md) |

Moderation and retention-exception specifics still deferred, not blocking | [ADR 0010](decisions/0010-public-gallery-visibility-flag.md) |

**Summary**: 9 of 19 fully defined (now including implemented-and-verified ones like row 16 and row 19), 6 partially, 4 not started. None of the remaining rows block writing the backend skeleton (providers/registry/job submit+poll) — they block specific features (real top-up, a full admin UI, push notifications) that get built after the skeleton runs end-to-end.
