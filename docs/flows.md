| 20 | Background job execution (Postgres-native queue, per-provider concurrency, Kie submit/track split) | **Implemented and verified for TTS, voice cloning, and Kie.** TTS/cloning: real evidence forced the original decision (the request-scoped DB session was held open for the whole provider round-trip against a default 15-connection pool, and Fish Audio's own account caps concurrency at 5, observed in a real response header) — `202 pending` in milliseconds, real pickup verified. **Since ADR 0026**, dispatch itself moved off Redis/arq to `SELECT ... The retry/backoff path (`jobs.tries`/`run_after`) was exercised by a genuine Fish Audio 500 (not simulated), correctly retrying twice then writing a real `failed` state | [ADR 0014](decisions/0014-background-job-execution.md), [ADR 0015](decisions/0015-kie-model-catalog.md), [ADR 0026](decisions/0026-postgres-native-job-queue.md), [architecture.md §3f](architecture.md) |
